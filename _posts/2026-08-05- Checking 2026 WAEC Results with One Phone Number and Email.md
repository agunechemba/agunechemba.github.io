# Checking 2026 WAEC Results for Multiple Candidates

If you are a school coordinator, teacher, or guardian checking 2026 WASSCE results for a cohort (e.g., 26 candidates), you might want to simplify the process by routing all One-Time Passwords (OTPs) to a single phone number and email address.

**The short answer:**  
* **No** for retrieving PINs and Serial Numbers.  
* **Yes** for viewing and printing the actual results.

---

## 1. Retrieving PINs & Serial Numbers

Physical smart cards are discontinued. Candidates must retrieve virtual Result Checker PINs and Serial Numbers online via [serial-pin.waec.org](https://serial-pin.waec.org).

### Required Information per Candidate
* **10-digit Examination Number**
* **11-digit National Identification Number (NIN)** used during registration
* **Phone number and email address** for receiving the authentication OTP

### Why You Should Avoid Using One Phone Number for All Candidates
* **Profile Matching:** WAEC cross-references contact details against candidate registration profiles.
* **Security & System Blocks:** Requesting multiple consecutive OTPs on one device triggers SMS suppression, anti-spam throttling, and IP blocks.

> **Recommendation:** Use each candidate's registered contact information, or coordinate directly with them to collect OTPs in real-time.

---

## 2. Checking and Printing Results

Once you have gathered the 12-digit PINs and Serial Numbers, accessing the main result portal at [waecdirect.org](https://waecdirect.org) is straightforward:

* **No OTPs Required:** Viewing results does not require phone or email validation.
* **Single Device Access:** A single administrator can sequentially check, save as PDF, and print results for all candidates from one computer.

---

## Official Bulk Processing Solutions

For institutional needs, WAEC offers dedicated bulk tools:

| Platform | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- |
| **[request.waec.ng](https://request.waec.ng)** | Bulk token purchases managed under a single administrative account. |

---

## Key Takeaway

> You **cannot** use a single phone number for multiple PIN retrievals on the retrieval portal without hitting system blocks. However, once you secure the individual PINs, you can check and print all results from a single device on the main WAEC portal without restrictions.
