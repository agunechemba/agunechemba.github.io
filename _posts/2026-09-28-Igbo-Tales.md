## What's Inside

Each volume now contains **28 stories** — the original 13 tales plus 15 newly researched and retold stories drawn from the traditional Igbo oral canon, ancient articles, books, and community forums.

The collections span:

- **Folktales & Animal Fables** — the Tortoise, the Leopard, the Python, the Elephant, and the whole animal kingdom
- **History & Heroism** — Jaja of Opobo, King Koko, the Women's War, Achebe, Azikiwe, Ojukwu, and the heroes of Igbo resistance
- **Spirits, Oracles & Sacred Things** — Chukwu, Ala, Amadioha, the Long Juju, the ofo, the ikenga, the kolanut, and the rites of the ancestors
- **Community & Custom** — market days, taboos, sacred ceremonies, and the laws that bind the living to the dead
- **Women Who Ruled** — the Omu, the Umuada, Ahebi Ugbabe, and the women who shaped Igbo society
- **Ancient Communities** — Nri, Igbo-Ukwu, Arochukwu, Onitsha, Nnewi, and the cradle of Igbo civilization
- **Dreams & Visions** — the *nrọ* that speak, the warnings of the night, and the visions of the dibia
