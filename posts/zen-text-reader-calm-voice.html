A minimalist, calm text-to-speech reader. Paste any text and it reads it aloud with natural punctuation pauses and a human-like voice.

Features

  • Paste & Listen — Drop in an article, chapter, or notes and hit play.
  • Calm Voice — Defaults to natural, soothing system voices (Samantha, Karen, Moira, etc.).
  • Punctuation Aware — Text is chunked by sentence so pauses feel natural.
  • Speed & Pitch — Fine-tune the voice to your liking.
  • Resume Anywhere — Progress is saved locally; pick up where you left off.
  • Keep Awake — Prevents your screen from sleeping during long sessions.
  • Offline Ready — Service worker caches assets for offline use.

Browser Support

Works best in Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox on desktop and mobile. Voice availability depends on your operating system.
