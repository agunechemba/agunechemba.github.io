Back in August 2022, one of my students, Adeleye Karyn, built a charming little portfolio page that deserves a spotlight. The project is a simple but heartfelt introduction to who she is and what she dreams of becoming.

Meet the "Tripple K"

The site opens with a warm "HI Viewers" and introduces Adeleye as a singer, dancer, and aspiring Hollywood actress—hence the "Tripple K" nickname. She shares that she is 11 years old and describes herself as "very creative, independent" and someone who loves to "let my dreams go wild." In her free time, she enjoys dancing Hip Hop and drawing.

Why This Project Matters

What makes this project special isn't its complexity—it's the unfiltered honesty and ambition of an 11-year-old putting her dreams on the internet. Building even a simple portfolio page requires courage: you have to decide who you are, what you love, and how you want the world to see you. Adeleye did all of that with confidence and personality.

The project is hosted on GitHub Pages, which means she also took her first steps into web publishing and version control—skills that many adults are still learning.

Looking Back, Looking Forward

Projects like this are a reminder that the best way to learn is to build something that matters to you. Adeleye didn't wait until she had "enough" skills or a "perfect" design. She started with who she was and what she loved—and that's exactly the right place to begin.

To Adeleye: Keep dreaming wild, keep dancing, and keep building. The world needs more creators like you. 🌟
